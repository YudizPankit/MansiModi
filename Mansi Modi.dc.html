Lineage

Disciple of Padma Vibhushan Kumudini Lakhia, Kadamb Centre for Dance.

Her journey with Kadamb Centre for Dance began as a disciple of Padma Vibhushan Kumudini Lakhia and evolved into roles as a faculty member and professional performer with the Kadamb Performing Unit, reflecting her deep understanding of Kathak.

Alongside this, Mansi has led D-Dancing Street for over 16 years as a choreographer and senior faculty for Belly Dance.

She has represented Indian culture internationally through classical and folk dance and actively promotes cross-cultural performing arts initiatives.

With expertise in teaching technique, choreographing culturally rich performances, and creating thematic productions, Mansi brings together creative vision and entrepreneurial excellence.
